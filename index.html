Account Deletion Request

Request deletion of your PocketPilot account and related personal data.

PocketPilot respects your privacy. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

What will be deleted?

  • Your PocketPilot account
  • Profile information
  • Expense records
  • Income records
  • Budgets
  • Subscriptions
  • Bill reminders
  • Savings goals
  • Debt planning data
  • AI finance chat history
  • Receipt scan data
  • App-specific personal preferences

What may be retained temporarily?

  • Crash logs for debugging (up to 30 days)
  • Billing/payment records where legally required
  • Security audit logs where required for fraud prevention
Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Deletion Processing Time

Account deletion requests are typically processed within 7 business days.
